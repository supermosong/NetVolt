Design Notes
1

7 Steps (3 out + pause + 3 back)

Device→Router→Modem→Internet (pause at cloud) → Modem→Router→Device. Each segment is one step.

2

Glowing packet dot

A pulsing circle travels along each line segment via requestAnimationFrame in JS, interpolating between node x-positions.

3

Active line lights up

The currently-traveling segment glows in its accent colour (cyan for radio, lime for cable, purple for fibre). Others stay dim.

4

Node border pulses at arrival

When the packet arrives at a node, its border briefly brightens and scales up, then settles — giving satisfying visual feedback.

5

Click any node or dot to jump

Clicking a node or progress dot jumps directly to that step, snapping the packet into position instantly.

6

Pause / Play / Speed / Restart

Pause freezes the packet mid-segment. Speed cycles 1×→2×→0.5×. Restart sends the packet back to Device.
